About Me

A student building a foundation in technology

My name is Tyler Kopp, and I am pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology at the University of Central Florida, where I am part of the UCF Cybersecurity Program. Before transferring to UCF, I earned my Associate in Arts from Seminole State College.

I enjoy learning how technology works, from the cables, protocols, and networks underneath it to the ways people and businesses rely on it every day. My strongest interests are cybersecurity, networking, and using automation to make business systems more reliable.

Outside the classroom, I work as a Marketing & Client Outreach Coordinator at a mortgage branch, where I connect business systems, maintain the branch's web presence, and automate client follow-ups. That work has shown me how much organizations depend on technology that is secure, well documented, and dependable.
