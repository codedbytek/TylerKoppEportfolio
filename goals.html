Professional Goals

Where I am headed

My career goals after graduating from UCF and the kind of cybersecurity work I want to do.

After Graduation

Building a career in cybersecurity

After I graduate with my B.S. in Information Technology in 2027, my goal is to begin my career in an entry-level cybersecurity role, such as a security operations center (SOC) analyst or a network security analyst. These roles focus on monitoring networks, reviewing logs and alerts, and investigating suspicious activity, which builds directly on the packet analysis and troubleshooting skills I developed in CNT 3004.

Over time, I want to grow into security engineering, where I can design and implement the controls that protect an organization, such as network segmentation, access management, and secure system integrations. I am especially interested in helping small and mid-sized businesses. Many of them handle sensitive financial and personal data but do not have a dedicated security team.

Why this path fits me

My work at a mortgage branch has shown me how much a business depends on connected systems, including its CRM, loan software, website, and automated communications. Every integration I build makes me think about who can access the data, how it moves between systems, and what would happen if something failed or was compromised. Cybersecurity lets me combine my interest in technology with work that protects people and businesses.

My next steps

  • Earn CompTIA Security+

    Build on my IT Fundamentals Pro certification with a widely recognized entry-level security credential.

  • Keep Building Hands-On Skills

    Continue practicing with Wireshark, Linux virtual machines, and home lab projects.

  • Gain Security Experience

    Pursue internships and projects that involve real monitoring, access control, and incident response work.

  • Finish Strong at UCF

    Complete my degree and the UCF Cybersecurity Program while maintaining Dean's List performance.
